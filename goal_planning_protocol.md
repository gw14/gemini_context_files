### **The Goal Planning Protocol**

#### **I. The Discovery Phase (Questionnaire)**

To plan a new goal, the system must be interrogated to ensure the "architecture" is sound. Follow these guiding constraints during the discovery process:

* **Sequential Interrogation:** Ask only one question at a time.
* **Engagement:** Wait for a specific response before moving to the next question.
* **Succinctness:** Provide only the question itself, removing background reasoning or flavor text to minimize cognitive load.

**The Question List:**

1. **Identity:** Who is the person you want to become through this goal?
2. **Process:** What is the specific daily or weekly process (not the result) that leads to this?
3. **The 2-Minute Rule:** How can you scale the start of this habit down to under 120 seconds?
4. **Environmental Design:** How will you change your physical/digital workspace to make the first step "obvious"?
5. **Habit Stacking:** What existing routine will you anchor this behavior to?
6. **Pre-Mortem:** Based on your current load (Go, CKA, Book), exactly how is this system likely to break?
7. **The Minimum Viable Day:** What is the 15-minute "Safe Mode" version for your busiest days?
8. **Energy Alignment:** Does this task require "Peak" brainpower or "Trough" (tired) energy?
9. **Integration:** How does this serve your long-term vision or career architecture?
10. **Friction Audit:** What is one physical or digital friction point we can remove right now?

---

#### **II. Case Study: The "Fiverr Freelancer" System Map**

*This example demonstrates the protocol applied to transitioning into a full-time Go/Python business owner.*

* **Identity:** A Business Owner who controls their time to provide for their family.
* **The Process:** Opening the task list every night to manage the business.
* **The 2-Minute Habit:** Opening the task list after dinner.
* **Environmental Design:** Pinned browser tabs for Fiverr and the task list to make the start "obvious."
* **Safe Mode (15-min version):** On high-load days (overloaded with CKA or Go), simply looking at the daily tasks to maintain momentum.
* **Energy Management:** This is a "Trough" energy task performed when the brain is tired after the primary technical sprints.
* **Integration:** Expands income and creates a functioning business, eventually giving back time for family and the Go Mastery book.

---

#### **III. Maintenance & Scaling**

* **The Weekly Review:** Use a weekly audit to update the "code" of the system if life circumstances change.
* **Friction Audits:** If you stop following the protocol, identify the digital or physical friction that stopped you rather than blaming willpower.
* **Never Miss Twice:** Perfection is not the goal, but a "double miss" is the start of a bad habit; get back on track immediately the next day.
