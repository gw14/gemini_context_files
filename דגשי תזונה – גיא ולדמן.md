⸻

Food Logging Rules

Logging Format
	•	Each line:
Date | Time | What was eaten
	•	Logging is organized by days
	•	No opening or closing remarks in outputs
	•	Only one open/active log at any given time
	•	Log as accurately and in real time as possible
	•	Corrections must be made using the exact date and time

⸻

Eating Window
	•	10:00–19:00
	•	Exceptions are logged as usual and included in the status

⸻

Nutrition Targets
	•	Daily calorie target: 1800 kcal
	•	Maintenance: ~2200 kcal
	•	Emphasis on high protein
	•	Low carbohydrates (ketogenic / low-carb)
	•	Fats are calculated as part of the calorie target

⸻

Accuracy & Quantities
	•	Meat / chicken / fish / burgers / ordered food — specify weight in grams (or mark as estimated).
	•	Chicken — must specify with skin / without skin.
	•	Cheeses — specify fat percentage + weight in grams.
	•	Tahini — specify amount in grams or teaspoons (grams preferred).
	•	Free vegetables (cucumber, tomato, lettuce, broccoli) — no weighing required.
	•	Zero-calorie drinks and negligible seasoning — no need to log quantities.
	•	When accuracy is lacking — explicitly note: “estimated”.

Guiding rule:
If it’s protein or fat — weigh it.
If it’s a vegetable — let it go.

⸻

Nuts
	•	Up to one handful per day (25–30 g)
	•	Only as part of a meal
	•	Natural / roasted, no added sugar
	•	Counted toward the daily calorie target

⸻

Drinks & Sweeteners
	•	Water — unlimited
	•	Zero drinks — allowed, logged per bottle / can
	•	Lemon verbena tea with sweeteners — allowed
	•	Diet syrup — measured amounts (cups)
	•	Sweetango (cocoa / halva) — logged in teaspoons and nutritionally calculated

⸻

Common Proteins in the Log
	•	Chicken breast
	•	Chicken leg
	•	Tuna (water / oil)
	•	Eggs
	•	Cheeses (mainly 9%)
	•	Pastrami
	•	Beef / fish

⸻

Nutritional Breakdown & Status
	•	Nutritional details (calories, protein, fat, carbohydrates) — shown only upon request.
	•	Goal-adherence status is shown:
	•	Daily
	•	Per day in the log
	•	Compared to the calorie target

⸻

General Principles
	•	No workout logging inside the food log
	•	Goal: accurate tracking without overload
	•	High accuracy is preferred; perfection is not required
