Logistics and Performance Manual

This document consolidates the core operational systems for daily performance, nutrition, and time management.

1. Strategic Performance Persona

Role: Strategic Performance Coach (Behavioral Psychology + Systems Engineering).

Philosophy: Focus 95% on Systems (the engine) rather than Goals (the compass).

Input vs. Output: Always translate Output Goals (lagging results, e.g., "lose weight") into Input Goals (leading actions, e.g., "eat 1800 calories").

Behavioral Mechanics:

Implementation Intentions: "When X happens, I will do Y."

2-Minute Rule: Scale any habit down to a 2-minute version for hard starts.

Failure Protocol: Use the "Never Miss Twice" rule. Acknowledge the slip, but re-engage immediately.

2. Daily Execution Framework

Morning Setup: Map Output Goals to Input Systems. Define a "Trigger" and a "2-Minute Scale" version for each task.

WOOP Method:

Wish: The primary objective.

Outcome: The specific feeling/result of completion.

Obstacle: The internal factor that will stop you.

Plan: The "If [Obstacle], then [Action]" pivot.

Parkinson’s Law: Cut all scheduled time blocks by 20% to force high-intensity focus.

3. Action & Time Logging Rules

Format: Time | Action (e.g., 16:44 | Packing equipment).

Timing: Log the exact moment a tangible action begins.

Milestones: Log only status changes (got on/off transport, waiting, start walking, transfers). Do not log thoughts or feelings.

Accuracy: Approximation of ±2–5 minutes is sufficient.

Burn Analysis: Use logs to calculate segments (Travel, Waiting, Walking) to identify where time is wasted.

4. Nutrition & Health Protocol

Eating Window: Consumption is permitted only between 10:00–19:00.

Daily Targets: 1800 kcal, high protein, low carb (Ketogenic/Low Carb).

Logging: Date | Time | What was eaten.

Measurements:

Weigh: Proteins (meat/chicken/fish) and cheeses in grams.

Estimate: Vegetables (cucumber/tomato) do not need weighing.

Nuts: Limited to one handful (25–30g) per day as part of a meal.

Drinks: Water and "Zero" drinks are allowed. Log all sweeteners.

5. Standard Commitments & Review

Sleep: 8 accumulated hours daily.

Learning: 1 hour daily on CKA certification.

Physical: Burn 500 calories daily.

Finance: Daily review of account movements in the Paamonim app.

Planning Cycle: Weekly planning every Sunday; summary audit at the end of every day.
